Overview

HIDEX is an organization with a dedicated software division. We build and maintain a small portfolio of mobile applications under one consistent organization, rather than as disconnected side projects — this site represents that software division.

Outside of software, HIDEX also operates in industrial oil tools supply. The two sides run independently; this site covers the software division only.

Approach

A few things that stay constant across everything we release.

  • 01

    Small team, direct ownership

    Every app has a clear owner inside the organization — no hand-offs, no orphaned releases.

  • 02

    Maintained, not just launched

    We treat shipping as the start of the work, not the end of it — updates and support continue after release.

  • 03

    Privacy-conscious by default

    We collect what an app needs to function and no more, and we say so plainly in each app's listing.
